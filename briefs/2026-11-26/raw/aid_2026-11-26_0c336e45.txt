How do you know when you've found an idea worth dedicating your life to? Trae Stephens, cofounder of Anduril Industries and Partner at Founders Fund, joins South Park Commons Partner Aditya Agarwal to share his unconventional journey from government intelligence to Palantir, venture capital, and eventually founding one of the most ambitious companies in defense tech. They discuss what makes a great founder, why sky-high startup valuations can be dangerous, and the lessons Trae learned from investing in hundreds of companies. Trae also reveals how Anduril got its start, why even the biggest ambitions require starting small, and what it really takes to build in defense, from navigating government bureaucracy to challenging the industry's biggest players.
