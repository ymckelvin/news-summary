How the AI Bubble Could Burst and Where the Demand Shock Could Be Part of today&#8217;s AI demand is driven by FOMO, from people believing they are getting good results. Diogo finds many buyers miscalibrated and overestimating what AI can do. Once they face the realities of its capabilities, demand could fall quickly. What Metric Did ChatGPT Optimize for in the Early Days? Before Claude Code, ChatGPT was optimized for monthly active users rather than the best chatbot experience, which is how sycophancy emerged.
