His advice for juniors Pick business impact over backlog : In pre-AI-coding 2011, Patrick McKenzie wrote “engineers are hired to create business value, not to program things.” Pick a radical idea : Come up with a few ideas that would, if successful, dramatically improve the chances that your organization achieves those goals. Be ambitious! Build it over a weekend : I have seen small groups and solo engineers fly past the usual process of writing product requirements documents (PRDs), getting buy-in, and lobbying for time with designers or product managers by building a working prototype. Focus on one good idea : In my experience, junior people often make the mistake of cramming multiple ideas into a single prototype , hiding three excellent ideas among 20 bad ones. Lean into your fresh perspective : If you’re working on something user-facing, you have a unique amount of empathy for users and thus a much better sense for how the product works or doesn’t work for them. AI Twitter Recap Genie 4: DeepMind's Real-Time World Model Adds Audio, Physics and Gemini Planning Launch : Google DeepMind introduced Genie 4, its "most realistic and interactive world model yet." Users can explore generated environments in real time and steer events, camera angles and actions with text ( GDM , Sundar Pichai , Demis Hassabis ). Architecture : The model is built on Gemini Omni. Gemini runs in the loop to plan multi-step events and keep scenes coherent ( features , Eli Collins ). Native audio : It generates ambient sound, music and real-time dialogue with characters, a first for Genie ( Shlomi Fruchter ). Physics and photorealism : DeepMind claims aerodynamics, collisions and natural human motion. Genie 3's worlds were "too static," and Genie 4 responds to prompted events mid-generation ( Fruchter ). Robotics angle : DeepMind positions Genie 4 as a step toward safe virtual environments for testing agents and robots. Demos : Gemini 3.8 Flash decomposes tasks, checks each step and replans as the scene changes. A Gemini 4 Argon + Genie 4 experiment simulates an Apollo humanoid playing checkers ( summary ). Availability : The research preview is open to trusted testers now. Google AI Ultra subscribers will get access next via Project Genie, followed by early-access partners through the Gemini API and AI Studio, which marks Genie's first API availability ( Logan Kilpatrick , waitlist ). Early-access impressions : Users with early access describe prompting a "neogothic brutalist city," then adding dragons and an NPC live. They call it "not flawless" but a large leap over Genie 3 ( Ethan Mollick , venturetwins , Riley Goodside ). AI for Math and Science: CFSG Formalized, ETH Disproof Claims Axiom formalizes CFSG : Axiom announced a complete Lean auto-formalization of the Classification of Finite Simple Groups. The original proof spans about 15,000 pages across roughly 500 papers by more than 100 mathematicians ( Axiom , Kevin Weil ). Process : An agent swarm produced about 50M lines of Lean in 24 days. Agents claimed tasks, reviewed each other's work and merged through GitHub, with human guidance, and reportedly fixed hundreds of errata in the published proof ( omarsar0 , kimmonismus ). Context : Researchers note it displaces the four-color theorem as the largest computer-verified proof. Few humans understand the full CFSG proof ( hyhieu226 , Stella Biderman ). Exponential Time Hypothesis : OpenAI announced new results in harmonic analysis, complexity theory and QFT, including a claimed refutation of ETH, with materials in its openai/math repo ( Romain Huet , Dan ). Anthropic's parallel claim : Anthropic reportedly says Claude independently disproved ETH, finding the key algorithm on Oct 16 with "14M output tokens and no human input." PSI also claims progress on five open quantum physics problems with computer-checked proofs ( Rundown roundup ). Physics follow-up : A Yang-Mills Working Group published a statement interpreting an AI result related to the existence of quantum Yang-Mills theory ( KITP ). Smaller results : Ten Claude agents proved in Lean that seven electrons on a sphere settle into a pentagonal bipyramid, a case of the Thomson problem ( Vals ). Separately, a Stanford study had 29 experts rate AI-proposed research problems and judged about one-third "interesting" ( Percy Liang ).
