What does it take to build a company that lasts? Snowflake CEO and former SVP of Ads at Google, Sridhar Ramaswamy, joins South Park Commons General Partner Aditya Agarwal to share lessons from leading Google's search and ads business, founding the ad-free search startup Neeva, and navigating the AI era at Snowflake. They discuss why Neeva failed, the founder trait Ramaswamy believes matters most, why he thinks most software will lose its moat, and what will still create lasting value in the age of AI. He also shares the surprising belief about search that he now thinks was wrong. The quality that separates the best founders is the the ability to strip away the noise and get to what truly matters.
