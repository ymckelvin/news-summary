Dario Amodei's IPO remarks (official position) Personal framing: Dario and Daniela lost their father to a disease that became curable a few years later. Dario cited CRISPR's decades-long path from discovery to treatment. Core thesis: Powerful AI working with humans "could compress a century of scientific and medical progress into a decade." He stressed that only early results exist so far. Named risks: Misuse by individuals or nation-states, economic disruption, and loss of control. Listed safety work includes bio/cyber safeguards, alignment training, and interpretability ("like an X-ray"). Key commitment: "We will pace the training and deployment of AI systems, taking as long as is necessary to have confidence that they are safe and will remain under human control. We will not bend to either business or political pressure." Strategic framing: The IPO "only strengthens our resolve." He restated the "race to the top," which should work "regardless of whether Anthropic is winning or losing at any given time" ( @AnthropicAI ).
