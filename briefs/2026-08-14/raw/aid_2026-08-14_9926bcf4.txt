The technique is somewhat described in the paper: Obtain a legitimate encrypted/signed reasoning block from an API response. Replay that block into a different request&#8212;potentially another account/session&#8212;to a weaker model from the same provider. Place it in an assistant/model turn and prompt or prefill the weaker model to transcribe the attached reasoning. Sample repeatedly, discard refusals, and optionally reconcile multiple noisy transcriptions. The paper gives concrete templates with some minor variations per model: Claude: replay the signed thinking block to Haiku 4.5, followed by an assistant prefill such as &lt;thinking-copy&gt; . GPT: inject the encrypted_content reasoning item multiple times into a fabricated conversation; sample up to 50 outputs. Gemini: attach thought_signature to a model turn with a &lt;thought&gt; prefill, then use repeated sampling and reconciliation.
