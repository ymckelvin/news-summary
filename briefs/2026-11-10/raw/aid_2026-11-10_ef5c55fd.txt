AI Twitter Recap Top Story: Claude Fable 5.2 / Mythos 5.2 Benchmarks, Economics, Restrictions, and First Impressions What happened Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, claiming both are "a step above Opus 5.5 in almost all the capabilities we evaluated." It tops most independent leaderboards. It also arrives with deliberate RSI nerfs, heavier per-task costs and restricted subscription access. Launch claims. &nbsp; @claudeai announced the pair as built for "the most daunting technical challenges," with gains in coding, reasoning and vision. @marktenenholtz flagged unusual system-card phrasing: "The model that underlies Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2." That implies a single shared base model. Vendor-reported headline numbers , per @kimmonismus : Up to 30% faster output than the 5.1 models. On FrontierCode 2.0, Fable 5.2 at High matched Opus 5.5 at Max for about half the cost per task, and at Xhigh it was the top released model. 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents formalized the existing proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours. Opus 5.5 completed 26% in 18 hours on the same setup. Mythos 5.2 accurately modeled 28 of 34 gene-therapy capsids. Alignment was at or above recent Claude models, with external pre-release testing. Default effort. Medium is the default effort in the Claude apps and Claude Code. Pricing. $8/$40 per 1M input/output tokens, 20% below Fable 5.1's $10/$50 ( @jaminball , @arena ). The rest of the spec, per Artificial Analysis : Cache writes cost $10 (5-minute TTL) or $16 (1-hour TTL); cache reads cost $0.20. 1M-token context with text and image input. Five effort levels: low, medium, high, xhigh, max. Long-horizon demos from Anthropic. An OS was built from an empty folder by 1,000+ subagents over 8 days. It boots on a laptop and has its own kernel, disk/keyboard/network drivers, persistent storage, windowing, login, an installer and built-in Claude ( @kimmonismus ). Fable designed three working electronic devices from a short prompt ( @kimmonismus ). It proposed untested chemical synthesis routes. It improved on listed solutions to 72 open optimization problems. @RLanceMartin published the safety-bounded scaling write-up for the 1,000-agent FLT run. Subscription access. Ultra and Team Premium users get Fable "resets" scaled by tenure: 1 reset under 6 months, 2 for 6–12 months, 3 above 12 months, usable until March 8 ( @ClaudeDevs ; echoed by @bcherny and @edwinarbus ). Pro and Team Standard get a one-week trial capped at half the weekly limit, plus one reset. Fable can only consume 50% of a weekly limit. Data retention. Fable 5.2 requires data retention by default so Anthropic's safety classifiers can run. Zero data retention is available only to eligible enterprise customers ( @code ).
