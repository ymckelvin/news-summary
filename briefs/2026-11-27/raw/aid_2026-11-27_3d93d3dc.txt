AI Twitter Recap AI-Generated Math Results: Matmul Exponent, Factoring and Lean Proofs Matrix multiplication exponent : OpenAI's model claims a lower bound matching its October upper bound, making ω exactly 9/4. Previously no nontrivial lower bound was known ( @SebastienBubeck ; repo ). Mechanism : The result reportedly comes through an explicit superlinear circuit lower bound ( @wjmzbmr1 ). Implications : Bubeck notes that a prior Lee et al. result implies LPs solvable in n^{9/4}. He also says LPs can now be solved in strongly polynomial time, a Smale problem ( follow-up ). Expert reaction : Omri Weinstein calls it "truly surreal" if correct, since most experts expected ω = 2 ( @WeinsteinOmri ). OpenAI batch : Nine new papers with Lean proofs came out a day after a claimed VP ≠ VNP proof ( summary ). Other claims : Fourier transform complexity of n√log n ( @lreyzin ). Results : A proven NFS-class factoring bound, beating a 1992 record. Also claimed: Deterministic subexponential factoring. Faster small-characteristic discrete log. A 1/5 Nash approximation. Progress below the square-root barrier on log-rank. Edge reconstruction for linear-density graphs. Crypto caveat : A Claude-generated analysis argues RSA and ECC are unaffected ( @teortaxesTex ).
