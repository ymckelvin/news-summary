More of the surplus from using intelligence has to stay at the firm, in weights, context or evals. He expects every firm to have a private eval benchmark as its core IP. AI Has to Inflect Global GDP Nadella frames the cost of training runs as a percentage of R&amp;D, noting that R&amp;D heavy companies spend 15 to 20% on it. Spending enough on training needs enough revenue, and that revenue comes from AI contributing to world GDP growth so that model makers can take their fair share. You Need to Have a Trust System Outside of the Model Providers That You Can Rely On With alignment not falling out of scaling and mechanistic understanding still elusive, Nadella says models should be treated within a trust architecture. He proposes an engineered zero trust environment that can contain any malfunctioning agent before it does harm. Will the Majority of Tokens Flow Through Open or Closed Models? Nadella expects there will always be a market for frontier models, with science possibly the next area where the frontier is set. Even so, he believes token usage will predominantly be commodity token usage. Why AI Does Have the Ability to Inflect Global GDP Nadella points to the deployment of Copilot in the NHS, saying that improving patient outcomes while reducing cost helps UK GDP. He sees pockets of the economy with the most benefit and least displacement, including software development, where demand has proven elastic and IT backlogs were huge. What Has Satya Nadella Changed His Mind on the Most in the Last 12 Months? Nadella believed in scaling laws in 2018 and 2019, when Dario, Ilya, Sam, Greg and Elon were all one happy family. His biggest change is that he no longer thinks alignment will just fall out of scaling, and now sees it as a harder, different problem. What Is Satya Nadella Most Excited for in the Next 10 Years? Nadella hopes AI diffuses across the world and all sectors of the economy faster than any previous general purpose technology, lifting everyone rather than producing winners and losers. Citing his father&#8217;s work as a development economist focused on convergence growth, he hopes this is what boosts the Global South.
