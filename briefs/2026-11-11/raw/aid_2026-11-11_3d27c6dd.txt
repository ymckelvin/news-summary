Gemini 4 Carbon is now live in Devin Desktop and Devin CLI.
On FrontierCode 2.0, our benchmark that evaluates models on real-world engineering tasks based on quality and mergeability, Gemini 4 Carbon scores 40.0%. That’s a substantial step up from Gemini 4 Argon’s 26.3%, and ahead of GPT-6.1 Sol’s 39.0%.
The improvement also comes at a lower cost. At low reasoning effort, Gemini 4 Carbon already surpasses Gemini 4 Argon’s best result, and its top score costs less than two-thirds as much per task as Argon’s.
