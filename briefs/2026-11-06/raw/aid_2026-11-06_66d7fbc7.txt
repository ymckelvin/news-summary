AI Twitter Recap Weight-Space Programming: Frontier Models Reading and Writing Neural Network Weights Core claim and replications : Several groups report that frontier LLMs can generate or edit small networks' weights directly, without gradient training or tools. Cross-generation replication : Alex Krentsel's replication of MIT's experiment found a sharp capability jump across model generations; the latest models generated Breakout networks scoring up to 22× an RL-trained baseline ( summary ). Hugging Face reproduction : Hugging Face reproduced the paper with ML Intern, its open-source AI-research harness, and released the model and a demo ( NielsRogge ). Hand-written classifier : A user had Fable hand-write a 100K-parameter network that classifies real versus fake legal citations, reportedly outperforming a trained version ( jacsebl ). Legora's CTO highlighted the result ( mstockton ). Astra's Qwen edit, reconstructed : GPT-6 Astra edited 131,072 weights in Qwen3.5-0.8B without backprop, raising MATH-500 from 7.9% to 48.1%. A Zhihu reconstruction from the logs shows the gain is narrower than the headline ( ZhihuFrontier ): Failure mode : 576 of 579 repaired answers had originally hit the 32K-token limit because of looping. Method : Astra derived a "repetition direction" from contrastive looping/productive contexts, located it mainly in the MLPs of layers 15 and 19, and subtracted 6× the projection from the top 64 aligned neurons per layer. Held-out result : Accuracy rose from 10.6% to 60.4% on 1,152 held-out problems. Most of the gain is existing reasoning that can now finish. Laptop reproduction : Simon Willison reports that Qwen 3.8 27B achieved the same weight-interpretation feat on his laptop after 45+ minutes ( simonw ). Skeptical readings : Baselines : Critics say the big claims lack competent baselines, the Qwen result is bug-fixing rather than recursive self-improvement, and the thread overclaims ( paul_cal ). Benchmark overfitting : Fitting weights directly against a benchmark is "essentially meaningless," as the nesformer Mario example shows ( dbreunig ). Mechanism : One thread asks whether models mainly read the first and last layers, which map to known inputs and outputs ( ravfogel ). Interpretability takeaway : Another view is that the real lesson is how effectively coding agents use mechanistic-interpretability tools ( jbhuang0604 ). Optimistic framing : Supporters frame the work as meta-learning finally working and treat weight optimization as structured-text optimization. They suggest hybrids that interleave backprop with direct edits ( yoonholeee ).
