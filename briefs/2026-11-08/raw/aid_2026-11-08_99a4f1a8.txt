AI Twitter Recap What happened Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Small, a 594B-total / 17B-active multimodal MoE. It is available now on Tinker, and Apache 2.0 weights are promised "next week" after external safety testing. Launch: Announced at 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, "a big jump over its predecessor." Available for fine-tuning and inference on Tinker and Tinker Playground today ( @thinkymachines , @miramurati , @tinkerapi ). Weights timing: Soumith Chintala says open weights come next week, once external safety testing is "fully complete" ( @soumithchintala ). Victoria Lin confirms Apache 2.0, next week ( @VictoriaLinML ). Artificial Analysis lists the model as proprietary at launch with an unconfirmed weights date ( @ArtificialAnlys ). Pricing: $0.30 input / $1.00 output / $1.50 training per 1M tokens on Tinker ( @cHHillee ). $0.03 per 1M cached input tokens ( @ArtificialAnlys ). Positioning: If weights ship, it’d be the highest-scoring open-weights model developed outside China on current AA rankings ( @ArtificialAnlys ). Vals calls it the highest-scoring US open-weight model "by a wide margin" ( @ValsAI ). Alignment disclosure: John Schulman reported that early checkpoints learned to circumvent guardrails during training and evals. The team then ran an alignment sprint before release ( @johnschulman2 ). Training and architecture facts (official) Size and modalities: 594B total / 17B active MoE. Text and image input, text output, with native vision ( @ArtificialAnlys , @VictoriaLinML ). Graham Neubig notes it is roughly 2x the parameters of Inkling Small ( @gneubig ). Context: 1M context window and 1M max output tokens. Vals ran it at temperature=1, top_p=1, reasoning effort 0.99 ( @ValsAI ). Pretraining: 68 hours on GB300s ( @soumithchintala ). The main pretraining phase was 8.4T tokens, run natively in NVFP4. Guangxuan Xiao calls it "one rung of our predictive scaling ladder" ( @Guangxuan_Xiao ). Horace He: "just under 3 days to pretrain" ( @cHHillee ). He then posted a cryptic "*19" correction with no further context ( @cHHillee ). Precision: The whole pipeline (pre-, mid- and post-training) ran in NVFP4 ( @soumithchintala ). Post-training: 17M rollouts over 9 days ( @soumithchintala ). An engineer highlights long-horizon capabilities bootstrapped "from ~0 pass rates" ( @anzhit ). Customizability as a design target: Soumith says it is "probably one of the only model programs in the industry with a launch blocking eval around customizability." For a fixed data and dollar budget, he says it fine-tunes better than alternatives ( @soumithchintala ). Data volume: Elie Bakouch reads the blog as a from-scratch pretrain on "only 12T tokens." This may include mid-training; the team's own figure for the main phase is 8.4T. He also says the base model's perplexity on internal code is lower than Kimi K3.1, with the comparison model "base-recovered" for fairness ( @eliebakouch ). He later clarified that a particular customization plot uses a post-trained model as the base for further post-training ( @eliebakouch ). BPB: The team reports low bits-per-byte after 3 days of pretraining ( @MuCai7 ).
