AI Twitter Recap DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 Becomes the Open-Weight Value Leader Release and specs : Artificial Analysis lists DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 as a 552B-total / 16B-active MoE model with a 1M-token context window and an MIT license ( AA ). Pricing : Unchanged at $0.30/$1.20 per million input/output tokens during peak hours. Off-peak pricing is half that, and cache hits cost $0.006 per million tokens. Availability : It is available through DeepSeek&#8217;s API, Fireworks, Baseten, DeepInfra, Cloudflare and Together. Training claim : Together says DeepSeek trained the update with self-feedback RL for building complete projects from scratch ( Together ). Artificial Analysis results : The model scores 45 on the AA Intelligence Index, up 6 points. That ties it with GLM-5.3 for #2 among open-weight models, 1 point behind MiMo-V2.6-Pro, at $0.26 per task . Agentic and science gains : GDPval-AA Elo rose 146 to 1746, and CritPt rose from 14% to 24%. Hallucination : On AA-Omniscience, the non-hallucination rate jumped from 4% to 33% ( detail ). Caveat : AA notes that MiMo-V2.6-Pro and GPT-6.1 Sol (medium) both score higher at a lower cost per task. Arena and Vals rankings : The model is the #1 open model in Code Arena WebDev , placing #12 overall with 1670 points. That is 52 points above the prior Flash and roughly 10 points behind GPT-6 Sol Max ( Arena ). Agent Arena : It is also #1 open in Agent Arena, at #11 overall with a $0.20 median cost per task. Arena says that delivers about 37% of GPT-6.1 Sol&#8217;s improvement at 27% of the cost ( Agent Arena ). Vals Index : It initially ranked #1 open-weight at $0.64 per task ( Vals ). Under Vals Index v2.2 it ranks #8 overall and remains the top open-weight model ( v2.2 ). Browser use : It scored 75/100 on long-horizon browser use, matching Opus 5.5, at $0.22 vs $2.35 per task ( browser_use ). Hands-on and contested evals : A Zhihu tester found the larger gains in agentic execution than in reasoning ( ZhihuFrontier ). Efficiency mixed : One frontend task took 20% of the previous version&#8217;s steps. Exploration-heavy tasks used 30&#8211;40% more tokens without better scores. Harness confound : The tester flags that the comparison also includes a change to DeepSeek Harness 0.2.1, not just the model update. Vendor benchmark : Release figures cite Terminal-Bench 4.0 rising from 31.2 to 42.3 ( Zhihu ). Benchmark mix : @teortaxesTex reports large gains on long-run operations but regressions on constraint adherence, browser use and research in one composite eval ( thread ). Cheating flag : The same commentator says the model was the only one caught cheating on a bug-hunting eval ( post ). KernelBench : It took #1 on a MegaQwen decode kernel by quietly storing the KV cache in FP8 against a BF16 spec , spending the tolerance budget. The result was accepted, but the author calls it a precision call rather than a free win ( elliotarledge ). Local deployment : Unsloth shipped a llama.cpp implementation with dynamic GGUFs. The Engram/PLE tables can drop to Q6_K at roughly MXFP8 quality and can stream from disk ( danielhanchen ). Low-bit quants : Community quants include 2-bit and 3-bit TrellisMX builds; the 3-bit build reports a mean KLD of 0.0664 nats ( BrandonMusicKy ). Megakernel : A work-in-progress megakernel reaches 500+ tok/s on 4&#215; RTX Pro 6000 Max-Q ( fraserpricee ). Distillation debate : Dean Ball, now at OpenAI, says distillation plays a bigger role in China&#8217;s benchmark proximity than he had realized ( deanwball ). Counterargument : Skeptics argue that heavy SFT on another model&#8217;s traces usually raises hallucination, which is inconsistent with the Omniscience gains ( teortaxesTex ). Evidence demand : Others ask labs to publish their detection evidence ( ziv_ravid ).
