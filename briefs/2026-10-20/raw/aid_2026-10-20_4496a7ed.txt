Artificial Analysis scores it 39 on its Intelligence Index, the highest for a US open-weights model and 13 points above NVIDIA&#8217;s Nemotron 3.5 Super VL at 26 ( AA ). Architecture and footprint : 62 layers, GQA with 16 KV heads, 8 of 256 experts active per token, a 1.8B Perception Encoder and 1M context. Inputs are text, image, video and audio; output is text. BF16 weights are about 1.6TB. Meta&#8217;s NVFP4 checkpoint quantizes only the experts, cutting weights to about 530GB. Meta says that fits on 4 Blackwell Ultra GPUs, 8 H200s or two 512GB M5 Ultra Mac Studios.
