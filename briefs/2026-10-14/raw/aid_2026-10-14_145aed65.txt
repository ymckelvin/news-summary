AI Twitter Recap Top Story: Meta launches Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full What happened Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL) shipped Muse Wonder (codename &#8220;Watermelon&#8221; &#127817;), its second-generation model family. Third-party evals put it near the frontier, roughly 14 months after the lab was founded. The run-up. Hours before launch, insiders posted watermelon teasers: @rpnickson (&#8221;It is a BIG watermelon&#8230;&#8221;), @alexandr_wang , @finkd (&#8221;Muse ordered me &#127817;&#8221;) and @EdwardSun0909 . @kimmonismus relayed leaked pricing from @synthwavedd: $4/M input, $20/M output, a 1M context window, and the max-reasoning &#8220;Wonder Full&#8221; tier exclusive to MuseCode Power/Maximum Usage plans. Two models. @finkd announced the family. He explained that Muse Spark was MSL&#8217;s first family and Wonder is &#8220;a much larger and more advanced class of models that brings us to the frontier.&#8221; The two releases are Muse Wonder (fast frontier) and Muse Wonder Full (the full model). Availability is split ( @AIatMeta , Newsroom ): Wonder is available via the Meta Model API and Muse Code. Full is available only to Muse Code subscribers. Wonder will power the Muse personal-agent product, subscribers first ( @finkd ). Muse Code installs with curl dev.meta.ai/install.sh | bash ( @alexandr_wang ). Positioning. Meta pitches the models as SOTA for &#8220;personal and professional agentic work.&#8221; @alexandr_wang named three headline features: Active Memory: memory across days, weeks and months. Active Focus: working through blockers to finish tasks. Domain expertise: SOTA in finance and legal, &#8220;strong in health.&#8221; Training story. Wang says Meta continued scaling both pre-training and RL since Muse Spark shipped in April ( post ; research blog ). @AIatMeta framed it as going &#8220;from rebuilding our foundation to pushing the frontier in 14 months.&#8221; Safety claims. @AIatMeta says the family was trained for robustness to adversarial instructions and for careful handling of private information. No detailed system card was referenced. Launch-day usage-limit bug. Wonder and Wonder Full were draining Muse Code quotas abnormally fast ( @alexandr_wang , follow-up ). It was fixed the same day, with a quota reset for all users ( fix ; @mjdouglas ). Wang noted &#8220;there&#8217;s definitely still issues we&#8217;re trying to iron out&#8221; ( post ). Media.
