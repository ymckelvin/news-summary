What Devin does on a Databricks migration
Devin, the AI software engineer, is now being used by enterprises to move legacy data platforms to Databricks. On a Databricks migration, Devin converts code from the legacy data warehouse, tests it in the customer’s workspace, fixes what fails, and reconciles every source against its Databricks copy before cutover. Devin can autonomously run hundreds of tasks in parallel, which is how one Fortune 500 energy company migrated a 142-object platform to Databricks Unity Catalog in just eight days. With data governed through Unity Catalog, enterprises can more quickly make their data available for analytics and AI workloads.
“AI is only as good as the context you give it. For some companies, critical business knowledge is stuck in legacy systems. With Devin and Unity Gateway, customers can easily move context from legacy platforms into Databricks, with everything governed in Unity Catalog, so they can start building AI that actually understands their business,” said Ali Ghodsi, Co-founder and CEO of Databricks.

A four-week project in two and a half with Zeb
A global digital transformation strategy company, Zeb, used Devin to move NetSuite data into Databricks, finishing a four-week migration in two and a half weeks.
“The question we ask on every migration isn’t how many engineers we can put on it. It’s what the highest-leverage team looks like for the outcome the customer needs. For a global investment advisory firm, Devin and our engineers built the NetSuite-to-Databricks pipelines and 20 tables, and we delivered a four-week project in two and a half,” said Mal Vivek, CEO at Zeb. “We’ve committed our Databricks delivery teams to Devin because it gives our customers more value per dollar.”
Why now
Migrations have been a major barrier between enterprises and the AI applications and agents they want to build. Devin for Databricks Migrations takes on work that used to tie up a whole engineering pod for multiple quarters and is able to parallelize tasks to accelerate project execution.
“We built Devin to own software engineering projects end to end. Migrations are a great example where agents need to understand the existing code, make the changes, and test that everything works,” said Scott Wu, Co-founder and CEO, Cognition. “This typically takes a lot of engineering time. With Devin for Databricks Migrations, we’re helping teams get through it faster so they can start building with their data sooner.”
