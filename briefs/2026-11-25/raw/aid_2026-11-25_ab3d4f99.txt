AI Twitter Recap Frontier Models as Research Engines: VP ≠ VNP, Protein Design and 948 Kernel Bugs VP ≠ VNP, posted by two labs : An Anthropic GitHub repo reportedly contains a proof by an internal Claude research model that VP ≠ VNP. This is the algebraic analogue of P vs NP, posed by Valiant in 1979, and implies that polynomials such as the permanent lack small circuits over every field ( @kimmonismus , repo pointer ). OpenAI's counter-release : OpenAI then posted its own proof and says it was "obtained on November 6, 2026." Neither lab named a model or published an announcement ( @AndrewCurran_ ). Scope : The result does not imply P ≠ NP, which still requires general Boolean circuit lower bounds ( @ZhihuFrontier ). Speculation : Curran argues that both labs are sitting on unreleased results and repeats unconfirmed rumors of cryptography breakthroughs. Treat this as opinion. Riemann proof clarifications : Levent Alpöge says the "250,000+ pages" claim was a joke. He released only the Lean code because he could not produce a readable writeup, and called it "full of garbage obviously, but compiles" ( @kimmonismus , @simonw ). Lineage claim : A Zhihu analysis traces Alpöge's key method to a short OpenAI proof excluding Landau–Siegel zeros. This is an attributed inference ( @ZhihuFrontier ). GPT-6 protein design, wet-lab validated : OpenAI released a preprint showing GPT-6 models designing new protein shapes and sequences through reasoning and code, without specialized bio models ( @OpenAI ). Results : 14 of 107 resequenced proteins showed binding. 128 of 182 redesigned small antibodies and 179 of 272 antibody fragments retained binding ( details ). Cost caveat : On a prior DNA task, GPT-6.1 Sol was about 2,000x more expensive per task than a specialized model ( @_lewtun ). GPT-6 Cyber and the Linux kernel : In one run, GPT-6 Cyber helped validate 948 Linux kernel bugs that could grant local root and generated patches for maintainer review ( @OpenAI ).
