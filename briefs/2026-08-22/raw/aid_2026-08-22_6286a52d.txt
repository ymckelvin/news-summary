Release: llm 0.32.1 Fresh installs of LLM stopped working the other day because the OpenAI Python library dropped its usage of httpx , and it turned out LLM depended on that library but only installed it via a transitive openai dependency. This dot-release fixes that for the moment by pinning to openai&lt;3 , and a soon-to-drop 0.33 release will switch from httpx to httpx2 . Tags: httpx , openai , llm
